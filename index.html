Helpful answers for students and families looking for tutoring in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru.

Which classes, boards and PUC streams does Pranamya Tutorials offer?

Pranamya Tutorials offers tuition in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru for Classes 8–10 under the Karnataka State, CBSE and ICSE boards, plus 1st and 2nd PUC in Science and Commerce.

Which subjects can students get help with?

For Classes 8–10, we focus on Mathematics, Science and Languages. PUC students can ask about Science combinations such as PCMB or PCMC, or Commerce subjects including Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics and Statistics.

Do you offer Class 10 and SSLC exam coaching in Rajajinagar?

Yes. Class 10 students can get board-focused support, chapter-wise tests, model papers and exam-preparation guidance. Karnataka State Class 10 is also known as SSLC.

Do you coach 1st PUC and 2nd PUC students?

Yes. Pranamya Tutorials offers 1st and 2nd PUC coaching for Science and Commerce streams. Contact us to ask about current batches and subject options.

Can we book a free demo class?

Yes. Families can contact Pranamya Tutorials to ask about a free demo class and experience the concept-first teaching approach before deciding.

How big are the batches?

Our batches are kept small—typically around 15–20 students—so there’s room for personal attention and questions.

Are study materials and test series included?

Enrolled students receive structured notes, question banks and exam-practice materials, along with regular tests. Ask our team what is included for the class and batch you’re considering.

How do parents follow a student’s progress?

Parents receive progress updates through regular test-score reports, attendance tracking and scheduled parent–teacher meetings.

Where is Pranamya Tutorials located?

Pranamya Tutorials is at #637/B, 1st K Block, E Block, Dr. Rajkumar Road, 2nd Stage, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru – 560010. Families looking for tuition classes near you in Rajajinagar can open directions in Google Maps ↗.

What are the class timings and tuition fees?

Timings and fees can vary by course and batch. Please call 96865 75508 or message us on WhatsApp for current details and available seats.
